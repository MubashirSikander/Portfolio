About Me

A bit about me

Flutter Developer with hands-on experience building cross-platform mobile applications for Android and iOS. I specialize in Flutter, Dart, Firebase, REST APIs, GetX, Riverpod, Hive, and Clean Architecture. I have contributed to production-ready applications with features such as offline-first data synchronization, role-based access control, secure authentication, and real-time data management. I enjoy turning business requirements into reliable mobile solutions while writing clean, maintainable, and scalable code. I work closely with designers, QA engineers, and backend developers to deliver high-quality applications that provide a smooth and consistent user experience.

I care about writing code that's easy for the next person — often future me — to pick up and extend, which is why clean architecture and clear separation of concerns show up in everything I build. I treat every project as a chance to get a little better: trying a new state management approach, tightening up an offline-sync flow, or simply writing a cleaner widget than I did last time. At the end of the day, the goal is always the same — a smooth, dependable experience for the person actually using the app.
